YR FurniTune — Privacy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

YR FurniTune is a private home layout planner used by one family. Only people the family has added to its member list can open it.

What we collect

  • Google sign-in: your name and email address, used only to check that you are on the family's member list and to show your name in the app. We do not receive your password or any other Google data.
  • What you add in the app: furniture placements, rooms, remarks, photos and favourites you create while planning.
  • A sign-in key on your device: a random key stored in your browser so you stay signed in (for up to 60 days). Signing out deletes it.

Where it is kept

Everything is stored in the family's own Google account (Google Sheets and Google Drive), through Google Apps Script. Nothing is stored on any other server. This website only holds the app's screens.

Sharing

Your information is never sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Other family members using the app can see your name next to the things you add.

Removing your data

Ask the family member who invited you. They can remove you from the member list (this ends your access straight away) and delete what you added.
